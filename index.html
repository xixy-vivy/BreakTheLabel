Break the Label

Stigma around health and poverty often causes more harm than the conditions themselves.

By Oluwadurotimi Durosinmi-Etti

Stigma Hurts More Than We Realise

Stigma is not always visible, but its impact is deeply felt. People experiencing illness or poverty are often judged instead of supported, which can make their situation even harder to manage.

This campaign focuses on replacing judgment with understanding and encouraging a more compassionate society where everyone is treated with dignity.

Understanding Stigmatization

Stigmatization refers to unfair treatment or labeling of individuals based on health, appearance, or social status. It often leads to exclusion and emotional harm.

Poverty and illness are often misunderstood, with people wrongly blamed for situations influenced by wider social and economic factors.

  • Misunderstanding of mental health and illness
  • Negative stereotypes about poverty
  • Social exclusion and isolation
  • Reduced confidence and self-worth
  • Lack of equal opportunities

Global Perspectives

Around the world, stigma is experienced differently depending on culture and education. Some communities still hold strong misconceptions, while others actively promote inclusion and awareness.

Organizations such as WHO and UNICEF continue to promote equality, health education, and global awareness campaigns.

Real-Life Experiences

Many individuals facing hardship feel isolated due to how they are treated by others. However, supportive environments can significantly improve mental wellbeing and confidence.
